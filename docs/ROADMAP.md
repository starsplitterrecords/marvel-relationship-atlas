# Roadmap

## 1. Preserve v0.4 and v0.6 baselines
Keep breadth-focused roster graph and evidence-backed interpersonal relationships separately until records can be joined with stable identity mappings.

## 2. Research breadth (top priority)
Rotate hourly among Avengers, mutants, street-level figures beyond Daredevil, cosmic empires, mystical networks, political leaders, families and villain coalitions. Prefer independent substantiated directional relationships to superficial membership edges.

## 3. Database integrity
Stable identity and source IDs, unique claim/event identifiers, provenance tiers, issue/era context, conflict resolution and review queues. Add exportable CSV and automated CI validation.

## 4. Presentation
Generate the mobile visualization directly from canonical committed JSON; support directed paths, filters, confidence, relationship comparison and source inspection. Avoid enormous undifferentiated graphs.

## 5. Long-term depth
Historical transitions, faction cohesion, formal-vs-informal power, full primary-comic panel verification, controversial continuity, aliases/retcons, and cross-period comparisons.

## 6. Deployment
Enable GitHub Pages, iPhone Safari and PWA testing, accessibility checks and offline smoke tests.
